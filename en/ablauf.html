How it works

You send us the property. We do the rest.

From your enquiry to the finished report – what happens, in what order, and why before signing is the right moment.

The situation

Buying a property abroad is a decision of its own kind.

When you buy a property in your own country, you can check a lot yourself. You can drive back at short notice. You know the language. You know how things are done. You know who to ask.

With a property in Turkey that is often different. The property you want is far away. A viewing may only last a short time. Photos do not show everything. And as a buyer, many technical or structural issues are simply not something you can judge reliably yourself.

This is exactly where we come in.

You stay where you are. We are where your property is.

The solution

Your property. Our eyes on site.

You have found a property you like. You can choose it from Germany, Austria, Switzerland or any other country – and send us there before you take the next step.

We drive to the property for you. We look closely. We check its condition. We document anything relevant. And we give you an independent basis for your decision.

Not to talk you out of the purchase. Not to frighten you. But so that you know better what you are getting into.

Before you buy, we take a look for you.

Process

This is how simple it is.

01

You send us the property

Send us the address, the listing or whatever information you already have.

02

We go there

We visit the property you are interested in, independently and in person.

03

We inspect and document

We go through the property with a thermal imaging camera, 3D scanner, drone and measuring equipment – non-destructively, nothing is opened up. Every finding is measured, photographed and put into context.

04

You get clarity

You receive the results in a form you can understand, and can then make your decision.

The right moment

Before you sign, let someone take a look.

You do not have to assume that something is wrong. You only have to allow for the possibility that you might have missed something.

That is what Fivestar Property Scan is for – so that you can make your decision on a better basis.

Found the property you want?

Then let someone look at it who is already there.
