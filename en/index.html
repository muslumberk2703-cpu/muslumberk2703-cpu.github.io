What we work with

We do not turn up with a pen and a notepad.

What matters most is often invisible during a viewing: damp behind fresh paint, missing insulation, a roof nobody climbs onto, or a floor plan smaller than the listing claims. That is why we bring measuring equipment no buyer carries to a viewing.

Thermal imaging camera

Makes temperature differences visible – indications of damp, gaps in insulation, leaking pipes and overheating electrics. Without opening anything.

3D scanner

Measures the interiors digitally – floor area and room dimensions to check against listing and contract, plus a walkable model.

Drone

Shows roof and façade from above – the most expensive damage starts where nobody looks during a normal viewing.

Laser & measuring tools

Delivers numbers instead of impressions – straight walls, level floors, falls on balconies and in showers, control measurements to the millimetre.

The technology is our tool. Your certainty is the goal.
