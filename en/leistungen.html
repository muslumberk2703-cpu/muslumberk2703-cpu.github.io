Thermal & infrared

What is behind the wall, the floor and the socket

The thermal camera shows temperature differences – including where nothing can be seen with the naked eye. On walls, ceilings and floors these can be signs of damp, missing insulation or a leaking pipe. In the same way we see whether the fuse box, the sockets or the connections are warming up unusually. Nothing is opened up for this. A single thermal image is not proof: we put every finding into context and write into the report where a closer inspection makes sense.

One finding, two measurements: the thermal image shows where something is unusual – a narrow straight line is usually a pipe, a diffuse patch points to moisture. Whether there really is water we verify at the same spot with the moisture meter, and set the reading against a dry reference area in the same room. Air humidity and surface temperature give us the dew point – that tells us whether it is water ingress or condensation. For you that is the difference between structural repair work and a question of insulation and ventilation. Measurement conditions and reference readings go into the report.

Construction monitoring & records

You see your home being built – even from far away

Many homes on the Turkish Riviera are sold before they are finished. The buyer lives abroad and never sees the building site. We take that over for you: we come by at fixed intervals, look at the state of the work and record what we find – with photos, measurements and a 3D tour you can walk through yourself. That way you find out early when something is not right. At that stage it can usually still be changed without much effort.

Complete documentation right through to handover

Why the timing matters: while building is still going on, pipes, waterproofing and screed are still open. You can see how carefully the work was done. Later it all sits under tiles and plaster. Anyone who only looks closely at the handover has, as a rule, already paid the purchase price – and getting something put right after that is considerably harder.

How it works: we capture everything digitally on site, analyse it at the computer and send you the report as a PDF. We travel in our own vehicle – getting to you within the region is included in the price.
