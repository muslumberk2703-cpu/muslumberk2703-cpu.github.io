Proportion

An inspection costs only a small part of your investment.

When you buy a property in Turkey, it quickly becomes a major financial decision. The cost of a professional inspection is only a small part of that total.

A single relevant defect overlooked before the purchase can later cause costs that are a multiple of an inspection – through repairs, refurbishment, technical rectification or consequential damage.

The question is not only what an inspection costs. The question is also what an overlooked defect might cost you later.

Included in every package

The inspection is identical in all three packages. Only the size of the property sets the price.

  • Systematic visual inspection of all accessible rooms
  • Thermal imaging: moisture, thermal bridges, unusual temperature spots
  • Dew point measurement – separates water ingress from condensation
  • Laser survey (±2 mm) – room by room, balcony and terrace listed separately
  • m² comparison against the listing and the purchase contract
  • To-scale floor plan with the measurements marked in
  • Straight walls, level floors, drainage falls in bathroom, balcony and terrace
  • Windows and doors including seals and opening sizes
  • Accessibility: step heights, door widths, thresholds, shower dimensions
  • Sockets and fuse box spot-checked with the thermal camera
  • Photo documentation of every finding
  • PDF report with defects ranked by urgency – red, amber, green
